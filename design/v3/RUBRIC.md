# Council rubric (fixed for every round)

Score each category 0–10 against the design intent in DIRECTION.md, at a best-in-class bar.
9 = excellent, ship-ready, a top product team would be proud of it. 10 = nothing you would change.
Be strict and specific; every point below 9 must come with the concrete change that would raise it.

Design (10):

1. Visual hierarchy — one clear focal point per screen; the eye knows where to go.
2. Typography — scale, weights, rhythm, line lengths, legibility.
3. Colour & state semantics — colour means state; nothing decorative; contrast.
4. Layout & spacing — grid, alignment, whitespace, density, section rhythm.
5. Consistency — same patterns for the same things across pages.
6. Brand distinctiveness — recognisable as Kura, not a template; ownable signature.
7. Clarity & information density — the right amount of information, nothing to decode.
8. Interaction states & feedback — hover, focus, active, loading, empty, error states.
9. Light/dark parity — both themes equally considered.
10. Craft & polish — no seams, clipping, misalignment, stray artifacts.

Plus (4):
11. Accessibility — contrast, focus, semantics, reduced motion.
12. Responsive — phones are composed, not shrunk.
13. Motion — purposeful, calm, interruptible, respects reduced motion.
14. Copy — clear, honest, consistent terms, sentence case.

Overall = your holistic score (not an average), same 0–10 bar.
